Need help?

Write to info@ruicodex.com with your device model and what happened. You can also use Profile › Community feedback inside the game.

Restoring purchases

Open the Store (or Profile) and tap "Restore purchases" while signed in to the same Google account you bought with.

Stuck on a puzzle?

Every puzzle is verified solvable. Hints place one piece correctly; when you run out of lumens you can watch a short video for a free hint (up to 5 per day).
